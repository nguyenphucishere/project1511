STARTING STATE (first frame): red alarm lights already flashing, cabin in tense red-orange light.

0-2s: the cabin shakes with growing vibration, red alarm lights pulse across the visors and suits, dust vibrates in the air, the helmets turn slowly toward each other.

4-5.5s: panels flicker and fail, a harsh orange fire glow floods the round window, the shaking gets even stronger, light flares across the visors.
5.5-6s: a blinding white flash swallows the whole frame.

Photoreal, film grain, shallow depth of field, high contrast, tense and terrifying.
CONSTRAINTS: keep exactly three astronauts with identical positions, suits and helmets as the first frame. The faces stay hidden behind the dark reflective visors. Do not morph or distort the helmets. No camera movement except the slow push-in and the cabin shake. No text, no subtitles, no logos, no music. The mood never relaxes.

"The cabin lighting changes in ONE direction only: from dim to darker to black. Never brighter."
và cuối prompt: "At no point does the cabin lighting return to its original brightness."

NẾU VIDEO BỊ NHẢY KHUNG (người đổi chỗ, mũ biến dạng), thêm:
